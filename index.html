      How to play & scoring

      The game: 11 rounds against 1–5 bots — pick how many on the game setup screen before you start (changing the Bots selector mid-game takes you back to setup). In round 1 you're dealt 3 cards and 3s are wild; each round you get one more card and the wild rank steps up — 4s in round 2, all the way to 13 cards with Kings wild in round 11. Lowest total score at the end wins.

      Your turn: draw the top card of the stock or take the face-up discard, then discard one card. Drag a card to move it, tap a card to select it, then tap a group box (or the hand) to park it there. Drop a card on the discard pile — or select it and use the Discard button — to end your turn.

      Books & runs: a book is 3+ cards of the same rank; a run is 3+ cards of the same suit in sequence. Wild cards (this round's rank) and jokers can stand in for any card. When every card left in your hand after a discard fits into books and runs, you go out — everyone else gets one last turn, then the round is scored.

      Scoring: cards that aren't in a book or run count against you at their points value. The deadwood pill above your hand always shows your best possible arrangement, and each group box shows whether it's a valid book/run and what its cards would cost you if it isn't.

      Suits: ★ Stars, ♥ Hearts, ♠ Spades, ♣ Clubs, ♦ Diamonds. The deck is two full sets plus six jokers — 116 cards.

      Playing online: choose Play online on the setup screen to create a private room and share its 4-letter code with your crew — everyone plays from their own phone, and bots can fill any empty seats. Or choose Play bots to play against the computer on this screen.
